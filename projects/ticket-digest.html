Ticket Order Digest

How are ticket sales and cancellations trending by market?

Teams responsible for getting ticket buyers to their events needed a daily read on demand and cancellations, both for the latest week and over longer periods.

What it shows

  • KPI strip: tickets sold, tickets cancelled (vs. the previous period), net tickets and sales.
  • Daily activity: sold vs. cancelled tickets per day, plus a daily detail table.
  • Time windows: 7-day, 30-day, 90-day, year-to-date and custom date-range views.
  • Weekly Cancellations: last complete week vs. prior, cancellation rate, and a 4, 8 or 12-week trend.
  • Market filter: every view can be narrowed to one market or event.

How it's built

  • Disconnected selector tables for the time window and number of weeks, read by measures, so one set of visuals serves every window.
  • Period-over-period measures that compare each window with the one before it.
  • Partial-week handling: the in-progress week is shown but excluded from "last complete week" comparisons, so early-week numbers don't look like a drop.
  • Tooltip page with daily detail on hover.

Data

Fully synthetic: 5,400 generated event-day rows across eight fictional markets, from January 2025 to November 2026. Covers ticket demand and cancellations only, not actual attendance. Rebuilt from a production dashboard with all branding and real data removed.
